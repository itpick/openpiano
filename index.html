Simple Piano (foxzi)

The curriculum skeleton. Vue 3 + Vite. 32 lesson pages covering a fixed 48-topic syllabus, 86 practices in 6 types (explore, set, sequence, rhythm, chord, ear), staff rendering, interval-by-ear training, RU/EN/ES, Web MIDI, additive synth, offline single-file build. Apache-2.0.

Simpli Piano (ConikerSystems)

The engagement loop. Static PWA. Dual course paths with an 80% pass gate, falling-notes engine (step + moving modes), star grading (90/70/40), real-piano mic grading (ACF2+ autocorrelation, onset detection, echo suppression), streaks, Daily Workout, 5-player profiles, anatomical hands overlay.

Simply Piano (JoyTunes — commercial)

The pattern both copy. Two paths (Soloist/Chords) + Lead Sheet convergence, ~28 courses ≈ "2 years of piano", MusicSense™ on-device TensorFlow chord recognition, 5,000-song library, printable sheet music. $150–170/yr, capped at early intermediate.

The fusion thesis

Take foxzi's content depth (theory, ear training, i18n, fixed topic ladder) + Coniker's motivation layer (paths, stars, streaks, workout, mic). Keep both apps' zero-backend, no-samples, offline-first ethos. Add what neither has: open contribution of lessons/songs.

      Learning mechanics worth copying research/*/ARCHITECTURE.md

      Teaching loop

      Show (falling notes + lit keys) → play (touch/keys/mic/MIDI) → grade (good/ok/bad) → advance. step waits for the right note; moving scrolls at tempo (±0.4-beat window, 4-beat count-in, metronome). A note hit after a wrong attempt caps at "ok".

      Mic grading (the hard part)

      Monophonic ACF2+ autocorrelation; rising-edge onset detection so repeated notes register; adaptive noise gate; echo suppression (ignore the app's own speaker for 1.2 s); unmatched mic notes are ignored, never penalized. Commercial Simply adds chords via MusicSense™ (on-device TensorFlow) — our open differentiator to chase.

      Practice taxonomy (foxzi)

      explore press N keys · set any order · sequence ordered · rhythm on metronome clicks (±0.35 beat) · chord together (≤1.2 s window) · ear interval by ear. Repeats: 1/2/3/∞.

      Motivation layer (Coniker)

      🔥 daily streak (≥60 s, local calendar day — never UTC), Daily Workout builder (5/10/15 min: reading warm-up → current song → chord drill → optional test), 5 local profiles, per-player namespaced localStorage.

      UX rules paid for in user feedback

      Hands overlay default-OFF · step mode auto-starts on first note · one sound at a time · keys capped at real-piano width (23.5 mm) · every row labels the hand · iPad Safari needs the aggressive SW update dance.

      Simply Piano's structure (from their own blog)

      The app = "menus" (native UI: onboarding, course map, library, sheet music) + "in-game" (music levels: static note sequences or moving songs). Lesson = recap → sub-lessons of 8–12 exercises → play-along song. Linear unlock; free replay of unlocked lessons.
